# Required Output Checklist

The following serves as list of required items for going to manufacture.

 * Gerbers
   * All copper layers
   * Paste Mask top
   * Paste Mask bottom
   * Solder mask top
   * Solder mask bottom
   * Drill legend
   * Drill
   * Silk top
   * Silk bottom
 * Pick and Place File
   * Top and bottom (.mnt and .mnb)
 * Stack up
 * Assemble Instructions and Drawing
 * Bill of materials (B.O.M)
